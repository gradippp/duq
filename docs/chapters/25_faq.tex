\section{General Questions}
\textbf{Q: Can I use Duq as a traditional compressor?} \\
A: No. Duq is a modulation effect that shapes volume via envelopes. It does not have a threshold, ratio, or detection circuit.

\textbf{Q: How many envelopes can I have?} \\
A: Duq supports an unlimited number of envelopes, though performance may be impacted by very high counts (12+). The first 12 envelopes support DAW automation.

\textbf{Q: Does Duq support MIDI Polyphony?} \\
A: Yes. If multiple MIDI notes assigned to different envelopes are played simultaneously, Duq will sum the gain reduction from all active envelopes.

\textbf{Q: Can I automate the envelope shapes?} \\
A: While you cannot automate individual point positions via DAW automation, you can automate the Rate, Depth, and Smoothness parameters.

\textbf{Q: Is there a VST2 version?} \\
A: No. Duq is built using modern standards and only supports VST3 and AU formats.
