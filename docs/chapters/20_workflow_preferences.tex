\chapter{Workflow Preferences}
\label{ch:workflow_preferences}

Duq is designed to be highly configurable to suit different production styles.

\section{MIDI Calibration}
If you find that the MIDI notes reported by Duq do not match your DAW's piano roll (a common issue due to different C0/C1 reference standards), use the \textbf{MIDI Octave Calibration} tool in the Settings.
1. Click "Listen for C0".
2. Play the note on your MIDI keyboard or DAW that you consider to be C0.
3. Duq will adjust its internal mapping to align with your setup.

\section{Editing Defaults}
You can set your preferred default Rate, Depth, and Smoothness values in the Workflow page. These will be applied automatically to every new envelope you create, saving time during setup.
