\chapter{Metering System}
\label{ch:metering_system}

The metering system on the right side of the interface provides precise feedback on levels and gain reduction.

\section{Input Meter}
Shows the peak level of the incoming audio signal before any processing.

\section{Gain Reduction (GR) Meter}
The central meter shows the amount of volume being "ducked" in real-time. 
\begin{itemize}
    \item The meter moves downwards as volume is reduced.
    \item The scale is linear, representing the current attenuation factor.
\end{itemize}

\section{Output Meter}
Shows the peak level of the final signal after the Mix and Lookahead processing.
