\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}

Welcome to the official technical manual for \textbf{Duq}, a specialized MIDI-triggered volume ducking and envelope modulation plugin.

Duq is designed for precision signal attenuation, providing a modern alternative to traditional sidechain compression. By using MIDI-triggered envelopes instead of audio-reactive sidechain inputs, Duq offers unparalleled control over the timing, shape, and transparency of gain reduction.

This manual provides comprehensive documentation on the plugin's architecture, user interface, and integration into professional Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs).
